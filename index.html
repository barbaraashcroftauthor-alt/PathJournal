Our work

The cover does the inviting.

PathJournal Publishing makes journals for people who want a place to think on paper. Some are guided. Things to Do While Your Phone Is Charging gives your hands a job when you'd rather not scroll. Others are mostly blank.

The Hand of God series wraps quiet, unlined pages in covers worthy of the words inside, leaving room for the prayer or the question or the gratitude list you came to write.

Either way, the cover does the inviting and the inside gets out of the way. We pick paper that takes ink without bleeding through. We size books to lie flat. The goal is a journal you actually finish, then start another.
